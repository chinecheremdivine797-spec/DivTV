# DIV TV Product Specification — V1

## Audience
Public viewers consume DIVSTUDIO content. They do not become creators.

## Viewer features
- Browse featured, latest and trending animation
- Search videos, series and categories
- Watch videos
- Like videos
- Comment on videos
- Share videos
- Follow/subscribe to DIV TV
- Save videos / Watch Later
- Continue Watching
- View counts and popularity

## Owner-only publishing
The DIVSTUDIO owner/admin is the only publishing role. Admin capabilities include:
- Upload video
- Upload thumbnail/poster
- Title and description
- Category and tags
- Series and episode management
- Publish now or schedule
- Edit/delete content
- Feature/trend content
- Pin and moderate comments
- View analytics

## Security model
Public users have no upload endpoint and no channel-creation workflow. Publishing routes must require an authenticated admin identity and server-side authorization.

## V1 commercial scope
Payments are intentionally excluded from V1.
